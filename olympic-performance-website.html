01 Data Preparation

From raw Olympic records to analysis-ready tables.

Step 1

Collect

  • athletes dataset: participation and athlete records
  • medal_results dataset: medals by country, sport and year
Step 2

Clean

  • Remove duplicates and nulls
  • Standardise country and sport names
  • Fix data types (year, counts)
Step 3

Join & Model

  • Relate the datasets on country, sport and year
  • Scope: 60 countries, 50 sports, 1992–2026
Step 4

Calculated Fields

  • Avg Medals per Athlete
  • Medal Efficiency %
  • Sport category and region groups
